# Call Center Analytics

A multi-stakeholder analytics case study for a mortgage servicer's call center, turning raw call volume and loan data into actionable insights for three internal teams.

## Deliverables by Team

### Product Team — Digital Footprint & Pre-Call Context
Analyzed customer digital behavior before calls to understand what drives inbound volume and where the self-serve experience breaks down.

### Operations Team — LLM-Driven Workforce Efficiency
Proposed an LLM-powered call summarization and routing framework to reduce handle time and improve agent utilization. Included workforce optimization recommendations.

### Compliance & Risk Team — Dynamic Risk Detection
Built a framework for detecting compliance risk signals in call patterns and verifying resolution across the loan lifecycle.

## Procurement Strategy
Each recommendation was accompanied by a build-vs-buy analysis and vendor evaluation framework.

## Methods & Tools

- **SQL** — call volume aggregation, loan-level joins, cohort analysis
- **Python** — data exploration, pattern detection, visualization
- **LLM integration design** — prompt engineering approach for call summarization

## Dataset

Two datasets: call records (`calls`) and loan portfolio data (`loans`). Raw data not included.
